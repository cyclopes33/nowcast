Understanding StormCast India

Convective-scale nowcasting is the prediction of rapidly developing weather phenomena such as thunderstorms, intense rainfall, hail and strong winds over the next few hours using high-resolution atmospheric and observational data.

StormCast is designed to provide short-term convective forecasts with a prediction horizon of up to 6 hours. The system continuously updates its predictions as new observations become available.

The system focuses on high-impact convective hazards including severe thunderstorms, extreme rainfall, cloudbursts, hail and strong downburst winds.

The proposed system can combine multiple sources including weather radar, satellite observations, rainfall measurements and numerical weather prediction data to generate high-resolution short-term forecasts.

The prediction system estimates the probability and intensity of different hazards. When predicted conditions cross predefined risk thresholds, corresponding warnings can be generated for affected regions.

The platform is intended to support disaster management authorities, weather agencies, emergency response teams, infrastructure operators and the public by providing localized short-term hazard information.
